Independent finance systems case study

Morvenesse

A traceable path from ERP-style source records to management reporting across entities, cost centers and projects.

An independent portfolio case study using synthetic master data, planning targets and transactions.

Live Demo GitHub Repository
The challenge

Management reporting needs a consistent view of accounting entries, cost centers, projects and budgets. Source records must remain traceable while accounting classifications, controlling rules and Work in Progress (WIP) connect them to meaningful financial results.

The challenge was to demonstrate that complete flow with synthetic data: show how the numbers are built, validate the underlying records and reconcile reporting outputs to their source postings without exposing employer or client data.

The solution

Morvenesse links synthetic master data and planning targets to balanced double-entry transactions and settlements. WIP valuation adds accounting entries with matching next-month reversals; account mappings and cost-center rules then route the enriched records into Management P&L, Actual vs Budget and project profitability reports.

Python is the source of truth for financial calculations and reporting outputs. Automated controls validate source schemas, master-data references, document balance and WIP pairing, then reconcile report totals across entity and cost-center views and project metrics to source postings.

The read-only React reporting interface provides navigation, filtering and presentation of exported results and prepared controls. Session-only demonstration entries remain separate from official results; React does not recalculate the financial reports.

Management reporting & financial performance

Management P&L

A management reporting view connecting monthly and year-to-date revenue, WIP movements, delivery margins and operating costs into EBITDA, EBIT and Net Result. The reporting hierarchy makes the relationship between business activity and financial performance visible.

Project profitability & WIP analysis

Project Intelligence

A project-level profitability view showing income, direct costs, WIP impact and the resulting bridge from core project income to direct project result.

Data model & reporting engine

Reporting Layer

Source data, reporting configuration and controls connected through a Python reporting engine that prepares structured outputs for management reporting modules.

SAP LSMW Case Study

A joint academic case study covering the preparation, execution and validation of a dependency-aware technical asset migration using SAP LSMW.

Full Academic Report
The approach

A limited SAP S/4HANA environment was prepared for an energy-sector scenario. Functional locations were migrated first, followed by equipment dependent on the existing technical hierarchy.

My contribution

I worked jointly across the full scope: organisational and technical configuration, source data preparation, LSMW setup, mapping, Batch Input execution, validation and documentation.

Academic case study & migration results

Migrated Technical Structure

The resulting technical hierarchy shows functional locations and assigned equipment after migration. In this joint academic case study, locations were created first and equipment followed their dependencies. The hierarchy provides a documented view of those relationships, connecting the preparation and execution of the LSMW migration to the technical asset structure created in SAP.

About

Finance-first expertise built on systems, data and automation.

My background combines financial analysis with practical experience across accounting operations, billing, cost control and procurement analytics. Moving through these areas has given me a broad understanding of how financial information originates, how processes affect it and how it can be translated into meaningful reporting.

Today, my work focuses on management reporting, actual-versus-plan analysis and ERP-based financial data. I use Excel, Power Query and Oracle NetSuite to work with reporting structures, transform source data and support consistent financial analysis.

I am particularly interested in improving the way finance teams work with information. Clear calculation rules, reliable mappings, reconciliation checks and repeatable reporting processes are important to me because they make financial outputs easier to understand, maintain and trust.

Outside my professional responsibilities, I develop independent finance-oriented projects that explore reporting systems, personal financial planning and practical automation. They allow me to combine financial knowledge with data modelling, application design and technical experimentation while keeping business usefulness at the centre.
